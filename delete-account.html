Delete your Sudoku Battle account

To delete your Sudoku Battle account and its data, email akgauttam552001@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Sudoku Battle account.

In the email, include:

  • Your player name, from Profile > Settings in the app
  • Your friend code, from Battle > Friends, if you have one
  • The Google email you used, if you logged in with Google

What we delete

Within 30 days, we delete your account ID, player name, avatar, rating, wins, losses, coins, season points, friends list and Google login details. Battles you played are removed from your account, and your opponents see them without your name.

What stays on your phone

Saved games, statistics and settings are stored only on your phone. Delete them with Profile > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.
