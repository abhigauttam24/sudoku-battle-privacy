Sudoku Battle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Sudoku Battle is a Sudoku game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information stored on your device

Your saved games, statistics, streaks, badges, settings and first-launch answers are stored on your device. We cannot see them. You can delete your games and statistics with Profile > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Information used for your account and Battle

When you use the app online, it creates a guest account for your coins and battles. We don't ask for your phone number, location, contacts or photos. We store these on our database provider, Supabase:

  • A random account ID
  • The name and avatar you choose, which other players can see
  • Your rating, wins, losses, draws and season points, which other players can see
  • Your coin balance and what you've earned coins for
  • Your battles, including puzzles, progress, finish times and opponents
  • Your friend code and friends list

If you choose Continue with Google, we also receive your Google email address and basic profile details so you can get back to your account. Google login is optional.

We use this information only to run accounts, battles, friends and leaderboards. Don't use your full name or other private details as your player name.

To have your account information deleted, email us at the address below and include your player name.

Sharing

If you choose to share a result, a room code, your friend code or the app, your phone's share menu opens. The message goes only to the app and people you choose. We don't sell your information.

Advertising

This version of the app does not show ads.

Children

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
